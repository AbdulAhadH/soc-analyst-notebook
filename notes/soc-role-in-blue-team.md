# SOC Role in Blue Team

Source: TryHackMe — SOC Level 1, Blue Team Introduction
Date: 2026-09-16

## Why this matters

Companies need to be secure to ensure day to day operation and customer information is not compromised.

## The teams, and what each one is for

- Red team — Attack, Penetration, Ethical hackers, looking for security issues
- Blue team — Defend, respond to attacks, SOC analyst
- GRC team - Managing policies and ensuring compliance with regulations

## Who sits where

CISO - Chief Information Security Officer
Team Lead / SOC Manager - Manages a team or SOC department 
SOC Engineer - Expert in configuring the security tool
SOC L2 Analyst - Experienced investigate more advanced attacks
SOC L1 Analyst - Look at alerts and pass complex cases to L2

## What an L1 analyst actually does in a shift

- Work with few security tools
- Triage attacks and pass complex ones to L2
- Protect the company systems

## When an L1 escalates

- When the identified attack or issue is more complex and may require more advanced tools

## What I got wrong or didn't know

- Role of GRC Auditor: PCI DSS audit
- CERT Lead : Immediate response from a ransomware attack on an office
